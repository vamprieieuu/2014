# 2014 Filter DNA

The creative direction is based on the visual culture of Instagram-era photography around 2014–2015, but all final looks are original presets and code.

Historical references show that the era's filters often used:
- warmth / yellow casts
- cool blue shadows
- purple or magenta shifts
- faded blacks
- low or high contrast depending on the look
- subtle vignette
- center brightness / edge darkening
- muted or selectively boosted colors
- softening

Instagram's 2014 additions included Slumber, Crema, Ludwig, Perpetua and Aden; in 2015 Lark, Reyes and Juno were added. Public descriptions of these filters are useful as **era references**, not as code to copy.

## Original 2014 city presets

### New York
Dark nightlife / flash.
- cooler-purple shadows
- warm skin/highlights
- denser blacks
- moderate vignette
- stronger low-light texture

### Miami
Colorful warm/cool split.
- warm highlights
- cyan/teal shadows
- magenta accents
- lifted blacks
- medium chroma grain

### Los Angeles
Sunset / soft social-camera look.
- warm skin
- purple-blue skies
- orange highlights
- softened contrast
- gentle fade

### Tokyo
Violet digital nostalgia.
- magenta/violet bias
- cooler shadows
- luminous highlights
- medium contrast
- visible but refined texture

### Paris
Soft pastel nostalgia.
- warm pink highlights
- lifted blacks
- gentle contrast
- soft detail

### London
Dark muted cool.
- lower saturation
- cool shadows
- dense mids
- restrained highlights

### Seattle
Cool green/cyan dim light.
- subdued saturation
- cyan/green cast
- compressed highlights
- soft blacks

### Berlin
Cool hard/desaturated.
- stronger contrast
- cool mids
- lower saturation
- defined grain

### Miami Night
A darker nightlife variant of Miami.
- violet shadows
- warm practicals
- cyan/purple separation
- strong but controlled chroma noise

## Preset philosophy

Every preset is a parameter state, never a baked LUT.

All filter parameters remain editable.
